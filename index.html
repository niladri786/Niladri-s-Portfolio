1

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about turnaround times, deliverables, revisions & workflow.

Standard turnaround time is 24 to 48 hours. For active creators with tight publishing schedules or breaking news videos, rush delivery (under 12-24 hours) is also available upon request.

You will receive high-resolution, full HD / 4K PNG and JPG files perfectly optimized for YouTube (1920x1080) and social media under platform file size limits. Editable Photoshop PSD source files can also be provided upon request.

Your 100% satisfaction is guaranteed. I offer unlimited minor revisions to tweak text typography, lighting depth, color grading, or expression retouching until your design stops the scroll.

Simply send your video topic or title, raw footage/headshots (if available), any reference thumbnails you like, and channel branding guidelines. If you don't have ideas, don't worry—I will handle the visual hook concept for you!

While CTR depends on video topics and audience interest, my designs follow proven YouTube visual psychology (high-contrast focal points, emotional expression enhancement, mobile-optimized typography) that consistently deliver an average 15%+ CTR boost for client channels.

My primary design tool is Adobe Photoshop for advanced photo manipulation, facial retouching, lighting effects, and composition, alongside Adobe Illustrator for vector branding assets and Canva for rapid social templates.
