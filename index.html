MY DESIGN PROCESS

1

UNDERSTANDING THE CONTENT

What is the video about? What emotion should it trigger?

2

FINDING THE HOOK

What will make someone stop scrolling immediately?

3

CONCEPT BUILDING

Sketching layout mentally (face expression, text hierarchy, background placement)

4

EXECUTION

Clean composition, lighting depth, high contrast, and impactful typography

5

OPTIMIZATION

Making sure visuals & text are instantly readable even on mobile devices

6

FINAL POLISH

Micro-details like realistic shadows, ambient glows, color grading & sharpness
